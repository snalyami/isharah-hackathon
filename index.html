SERVICES

One platform, multiple accessibility services

🤟

Sign2Text

Point the camera at signing and convert Saudi Sign Language into Arabic text and speech.

🗣️

Text2Sign

Type, paste, or speak Arabic and receive the information back in Saudi Sign Language.

↔️

Live Interpret

A two-way conversation mode for smoother communication between Deaf and hearing users.

🆘

Emergency SOS

Fast emergency communication with location, medical profile, and preferred communication mode.

👥

Interpreter Booking

Request a certified human interpreter for situations that need professional support.

📄

Document & Form Assistant

Scan a form or letter, extract key information, and explain it in signed video.
